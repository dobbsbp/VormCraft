Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

VormCraft is a free, open-source 3D modeling program made by Ben Dobbs, an independent developer. It runs in your web browser at vormcraft.app and as a Windows app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use either one.

The short version

  • There are no VormCraft accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • Your designs stay on your own device, or in your own Google Drive if you choose to connect it. VormCraft has no server that receives, stores or reads your designs.
  • If you connect Google Drive, VormCraft can only see the files it created or that you opened with it, nothing else in your Drive. Your Google data is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI.

What stays on your device

  • Your designs. Saving writes a .vorm file wherever you choose (in the web version, Save downloads the file). A .vorm file also contains the design's version history and its last undo steps, so you can go back after reopening it. To share a design without them, use Save a Copy Without History.
  • Autosaved recovery copies. In the Windows app they are kept in the app's data folder (or a folder you choose in Settings); in the web version, in your browser's storage for vormcraft.app. They are deleted after a set number of days (14 by default).
  • Settings. Your preferences (autosave, version history, whether Google Drive is shown and so on) are kept in the app's storage on your device.
  • Imported files. SketchUp, OBJ and STL files you import are read and converted on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.

None of this is sent to us. You can remove it at any time: delete your files, uninstall the Windows app (and its backup folder), or clear the site data for vormcraft.app in your browser.

Google Drive (optional)

You can connect a Google account to save designs to Google Drive, open them from there, and keep an autosaved copy there. Nothing is connected unless you choose to.

What VormCraft can access

VormCraft asks Google for one permission: See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (the drive.file permission). With it, VormCraft can:

  • create a VormCraft folder in your Drive (with an Autosave folder inside it, if you switch on autosaved copies);
  • save designs into it, update them when you save again, and open the designs it saved;
  • see your Google account's name and email address, to show which account is connected.

It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

How your Google data is used

  • Your designs and your account name and email are used only to show you which account is connected and to save and open your designs, as you ask.
  • Data travels directly between your device and Google. It never passes through or is stored on any VormCraft server.
  • Your Google data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not read by any person.

VormCraft’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sign-in details

VormCraft never sees your Google password: you sign in on Google's own page. Google then gives VormCraft a time-limited permission (a token).

  • In the web version, the token is kept in memory only and lasts up to an hour.
  • In the Windows app, a token that lets VormCraft stay signed in between sessions is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your computer, protected by your Windows account.
  • To remember which account to suggest next time, the email address of the connected account is kept in the app's storage on your device.

Disconnecting

Choose Disconnect in VormCraft's Google Drive menu: VormCraft gives the permission back to Google and forgets the sign-in on your device. You can also remove VormCraft's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party connections. Your files stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

Connections VormCraft makes

  • The website. vormcraft.app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address and browser when you load the site; see GitHub's privacy statement. VormCraft adds no tracking of its own.
  • Update checks. The web version checks vormcraft.app for a newer version. The Windows app checks VormCraft's GitHub Releases page when it starts; you can switch this off in Settings.
  • Google. Only when you use Google Drive: Google's sign-in and Drive services (accounts.google.com and googleapis.com). Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Links you choose to open, such as the GitHub project page, reporting a problem, or donating, take you to those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

VormCraft is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and important changes will be mentioned in the release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email info@vormcraft.app, or open an issue on GitHub.
